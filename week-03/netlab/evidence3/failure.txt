== Interpretation ==
With r1-r2 down, r1 forwards toward h2 through r3, but r2 reports no route back to h1. The traceroute reaches r3 and then times out, and the ping fails.
Static routes do not recalculate when links change. Bringing r1-r2 back restores the connected link but does not restore the static route on r2 to 10.3.1.0/24; connectivity returns only after that route is manually replaced.
